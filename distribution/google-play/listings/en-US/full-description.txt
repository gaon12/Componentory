Componentory is a hands-on learning app for Android UI. Search 168 catalog entries, including buttons, switches, text fields, and date pickers, and interact with the designs available for each component.

Explore design changes
Browse Classic, Holo, Material Design 1, Material Design 2, Material Design 3, Material You, and Material 3 Expressive. Each component identifies its actual implementation and supported combinations. Material You uses device dynamic colors on Android 12 and later.

Compare and keep a record
Choose independent designs in two panels and compare inputs and state. Save comparisons with the device OS, display settings, theme, and library versions. Reopen or delete saved runs, or export them to an app you choose.

Read version history and source evidence
Explore class descriptions and history based on public SDK and AOSP sources from API 1 through 36. Current-device framework widgets, pinned Compose libraries, and AOSP resource recreations are identified separately. Selecting a theme does not change the operating system. Missing original historical OS captures remain explicit. Toast previews are custom recreations using version-specific AOSP resources.

Navigate on phones and larger screens
Use layouts adapted to compact and wide windows, light and dark app themes, and a first-launch introduction. The app supports Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. Source notices and the privacy policy are available offline.

Learn offline
Learning works without an account or ads. Optional online game rankings use a Google game profile and Google SDK diagnostics. Preferences and comparison runs stay on your device. Some components require newer Android releases; unsupported combinations explain why. An optional keyboard and autofill demonstration on Android 11 and later uses a fixed fictional value.
