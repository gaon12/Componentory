Componentory Privacy Policy
Effective date: October 9, 2026

Componentory is an independent Android UI learning app maintained by gaon12.
This policy describes the app identified as xyz.gaon.componentory.

Local processing
The learning features, normal survivor play, and personal records work without
an account. Internet permission supports optional Google Play Games rankings.
There is no advertising, developer analytics, or developer-operated server.
Google SDK processing for configured online builds is explained below.

The app stores appearance and language preferences, introduction progress, and
comparison runs in its private storage. A saved run can contain sample text you
enter and technical context: Android version and build fingerprint, device model,
screen dimensions, density, font scale, orientation, locale, app version, selected
themes, and UI library versions. These details explain where the sample ran.
The app does not read hardware identifiers such as IMEI or device serial numbers.
Use example text rather than passwords or other private information in samples.

Optional keyboard and autofill demonstration
Android 11 and later can run a demonstration with a fixed fictional suggestion.
You choose whether to enable its keyboard and autofill service in Android settings.
The autofill service rejects other activities before inspecting their view trees.
It identifies the demonstration field without reading or saving its entered text.
The demo keyboard accepts only Componentory's marked demonstration editor; it does
not read, store, or send keystrokes from other apps. Return to your usual keyboard
and autofill service after the demonstration. These services are not password
managers and do not access account credentials.

Optional Easter egg games
AOSP-derived Easter egg ports store game progress, generated cats, cat names,
feeding choices, and simulation preferences locally. Cat visits can schedule
local Android jobs after you choose food. These jobs follow Android battery and
background limits and do not contact a server. Vibration follows your device.

Notifications for cat visits and spacecraft progress are optional. On Android
13 and later you choose whether to grant notification permission. Device-control
providers, the cat quick settings tile, color palette widgets, and screen savers
are enabled only through your game gestures or explicit choices. Select them in
Android or your launcher; they do not read or control unrelated devices.

Saving or sharing a cat can write its generated image to Pictures/Cats. Android
7 through 9 may ask for storage permissions; newer Android versions use MediaStore
for the generated image without broad media access. Sharing uses Android's share
sheet, and you choose the receiving app. Saved gallery images remain after the
app is uninstalled; remove them with your gallery or file manager. Emoji fonts,
framework icons, and the wallpaper-derived system palette use the installed OS.
The app does not send your wallpaper, game progress, or cat images to the developer.

Survivor records and optional online rankings
The survivor game stores its wallet, unlocks, common upgrades, unfinished combat,
and detailed records in separate private storage. Records include starting weapon,
mode, ruleset, seed, starting upgrades, final equipment and evolutions, survival,
kill categories, outcome, score, and earned currency. Normal play and these records
remain usable offline. Detailed builds and combat checkpoints are not uploaded.

Configured builds initialize Google Play Games Services v2. An existing game
profile can authenticate automatically. Automatic creation prompts are suppressed;
choose Connect game profile to connect or create one. Unconfigured builds disable
SDK initialization and online rankings. Google handles gamer identity, submitted
scores, and SDK analytics and diagnostics. Its service encrypts transmitted data.
Google controls profile visibility and deletion through Play Games and Google
account settings. See https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/data-collection and
https://policies.google.com/privacy for its data handling.

Only completed ranked victories and deaths queue scores. Failed submissions stay
in private storage for retry. A saved game profile ID keeps a pending score bound
to its owner; changing profiles does not reassign it. Offline scores without an
owner require your explicit Submit offline scores action. Abandoned runs and
normal runs do not submit. The app does not request Google passwords, email,
friends lists, or server authorization credentials. Google leaderboard records
can remain after uninstalling; manage their deletion through your Play Games
profile. Clearing this app does not delete Google's service data.

Exports and external links
Exporting comparison runs uses Android's share sheet. The shared text can include
your sample inputs and the technical context listed above. You choose its
destination; the receiving app may save or send it under its own privacy policy.
The app does not export automatically. External website links open an
external browser. Those services handle visits and any information you submit
under their own privacy policies. Public GitHub issues are visible to others;
do not include private information in an issue or an attached run.

Storage, retention, and deletion
Local data is protected by Android's application sandbox. App-managed automatic
cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. Comparison runs remain until you delete them
in the Runs screen or clear app storage in Android settings. Survivor saves,
records, wallet, and pending scores remain until you clear app storage. Clearing app
storage or uninstalling removes the app's local data and preferences. Copies you
export remain with the destination you chose; delete them through that service.
The developer cannot recover your local data or delete those exported copies.

Contact and changes
For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through
https://github.com/gaon12/Componentory/issues/new . Do not post sensitive details.
The public policy is available at
https://gaon12.github.io/Componentory/privacy.html .
Changes to app data handling will be reflected in this policy and its effective
date. The offline copy is available in Settings > Privacy policy.
